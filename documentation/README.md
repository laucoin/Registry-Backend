# ℹ️ Summary

## Scouts et Guides de France

The register was originally created (the first two versions) for the "Scouts et Guides de France association" (SGDF).

Its primary function is to provide a digital version of the paper registers (this is also the origin of its name "
Registry"). The SGDF is an association, and the aim of scouting associations is to educate young people.&#x20;

## Original features

The reception of minors is governed by rules. In France, one of them mentions that the director must know where are the
youth (and obviously when). Moreover, the project was born in the "Nature Environnement" (NE) part of the association.

At the beginning of NE, we protect Mediterranean forests against fire. This activity add some needs to Registry
application.

* Movements (with and without vehicle)
* Communications (radio and phone)

## Time evolution

The evolution of many things (our activities, people ideas and suggestions, my volunteer mission, my professional skill,
etc.) create many others needs and use cases for the application. This application originally developed for the SGDF now
require features which ones can provide a business value and make Registry sellable.

For theses reasons, and the technical challenge I decided to create a new version of Registry non related to the SGDF.

That a new start! A new V1!
